# Tone gate: night one with three outside readers

The brief makes this a stop-work check before the game ships: three people who did not build the game play night one, and their answers are logged below. The game is built past it, so this is now a check before submission. If two of the three answer question 2 with "funny" or question 3 with "bored", rewrite night one's beats before anything is sent to Play.

## The reader build

Night one is free in every edition, so no special build is needed. Use either:

- **Play internal testing** (once the 1.1.0 bundle is uploaded): add the reader as a tester and send them the testing link.
- **A browser, no install**: on GitHub, Code > Download ZIP, unzip it, and open `index.html` in Chrome or Safari. It plays offline. Nights one to three are open; the unlock sheet says it needs the Google Play edition.

Ask the reader to play until the six o'clock ledger of night one and then stop. Do not explain the game first, and do not watch over their shoulder. It takes 10 to 15 minutes.

## The questions

Ask them right after the ledger, in this order, and write their words down rather than a summary.

1. In a sentence, what was that night like?
2. Was there a moment that made you uneasy? Was there a moment that made you laugh? Which, if either, was stronger?
3. Was there a stretch where you were waiting for something to happen? Roughly when?
4. What did you think the rule sheet was, and did you want to follow it?
5. Would you play night two? Why, or why not?

## What passes

- At least two of the three name an uneasy moment (question 2), and laughter is not the stronger feeling for two of them.
- No more than one reports a long wait with nothing to do (question 3).
- At least two would play night two (question 5).
