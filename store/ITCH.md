# itch.io page — Night Shift: Laundromat

A draft for a free web demo on itch.io. The web build plays nights one to
three; nights four to fourteen are the Google Play edition, so the itch page
works as a free demo that points to Play. Nothing here is published yet: the
page is created by hand, as below. Keep the copy within TONE-GATE.md: uneasy,
not funny, and never sold on its mature themes.

The zip is `nightshift-itch.zip`, built with
`python3 itch_pack.py <repo> nightshift-itch.zip` (the marketing tools). It
holds `index.html` at its root plus the files the page loads, and nothing from
the Android, test or store side. In a browser the unlock sheet says the full
game is unlocked in the Google Play edition.

## Upload

1. https://itch.io/game/new
2. **Kind of project:** HTML. Upload `nightshift-itch.zip` and tick
   **This file will be played in the browser**.
3. **Embed options:** *Embed in page*; viewport **360 × 640** (portrait); tick
   **Mobile friendly** (orientation: *Portrait*) and **Fullscreen button**.
   SharedArrayBuffer: off (not used).
4. **Pricing:** *No payments* (free web demo). Never *Paid* here while the
   Google Play edition is the paid one.
5. **Age rating / content:** mark the page as having mature content only if
   itch asks; the Play rating is 16+ for horror themes in text. No gore, no
   sexual content, no flashing.
6. **Visibility:** *Draft* until the page below is filled in and checked,
   then *Public*.

**Description** (itch takes Markdown)

    **You are the only attendant at a 24-hour laundromat, 11pm to 6am.**

    Keep eight machines turning, the floor dry, the soap stocked and the
    change drawer honest, with a power budget that has to last until six.
    Serve the regulars. They are a little off.

    **The last attendant left a rule sheet by the register, in blue ink.**
    It gains a rule every night. Keeping the rules costs money or nerve, and
    breaking them sometimes pays.

    **The horror comes through the systems, not the dark.** A cycle finishes
    that nobody loaded. A name appears in the entry log while the camera
    shows an empty door. No jump scares, no gore, no flashing.

    **This web version is nights one to three.**

    **The whole fortnight is on Google Play:** all fourteen nights, ten
    regulars and six endings, decided by the whole fortnight.
    https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.taugustincst.nightshift

    No ads, no accounts, no timers. Progress stays in your browser. For
    players 16 and over.
