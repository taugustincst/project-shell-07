## Full description
**11pm to 6am. Fourteen nights.** You are the only attendant at a 24-hour laundromat in a town where the regulars are a little off. Keep eight machines turning, the floor dry, the soap stocked, the change drawer honest and the power budget alive until six.

**The rule sheet.** The last attendant left a sheet by the register. It gains one rule every night. The rules are real: following them costs money or nerve; breaking them sometimes pays. Two of them cannot both be kept.

**Horror from the systems, not the dark.** A cycle finishes that nobody loaded. A name appears in the entry log while the camera shows an empty door. A coin from a year that has not happened. No jump scares, no gore, no flashing. Dread arrives in the ledger.

**Two things to lose.** Cash and nerve. Many choices spend one to save the other. Six endings, decided by the whole fortnight, never by a single choice.

- Ten regulars whose arcs drift across the nights
- Forty-three authored beats, twenty table events, a line in the log each night that you did not write
- Save per night: restart the night, never the fortnight
- No ads, no accounts, no timers, fully offline
- Portrait, one-handed, large-text and high-contrast options, no twitch input, no strobe
