## Full description
**A slow-burn horror management sim: you are the only attendant at a 24-hour laundromat, 11pm to 6am, for fourteen nights.**

**Keep the shop running.** Eight machines to keep turning, a floor to keep dry, soap to stock, a change drawer to keep honest and a power budget that has to last until six. The regulars in this town are a little off. Serve them anyway.

**The rule sheet.** The last attendant left a sheet by the register, in blue ink. It gains one rule every night. The rules are real: keeping them costs money or nerve, and breaking them sometimes pays. Two of them cannot both be kept.

**Horror from the systems, not the dark.** A cycle finishes that nobody loaded. A name appears in the entry log while the camera shows an empty door. A coin from a year that has not happened. No jump scares, no gore, no flashing.

**Two things to lose.** Cash and nerve, and many choices spend one to save the other. Six endings, decided by the whole fortnight, never by a single choice.

- Ten regulars whose stories drift across the nights
- Forty-three authored story beats and thirty-four smaller events, and a line in the log each night that you did not write
- Save per night: restart the night, never the fortnight
- Nights one to three are free; one purchase opens the whole fortnight
- No ads, no accounts, no timers
- Offline game: plays with no connection
- Portrait, one-handed, large-text and high-contrast options, no twitch input, no strobe
